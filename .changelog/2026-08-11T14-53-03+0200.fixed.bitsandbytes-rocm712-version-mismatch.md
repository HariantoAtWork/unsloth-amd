Force bitsandbytes onto the shipped ROCm 7.2 binary when PyTorch reports TheRock/torch `+rocm7.12`.

bitsandbytes 0.49.x maps HIP `7.12` to library tag `82` (`major*10+minor`) and has no `BNB_ROCM_VERSION` override (it even rejects `BNB_CUDA_VERSION` on ROCm). No wheel ships `libbitsandbytes_rocm82.so`. Alias missing tags (`82`, `712`) to the gfx1151-capable `libbitsandbytes_rocm72.so`, and set `BNB_ROCM_VERSION=72` for bitsandbytes >= 0.50.
